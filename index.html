How often do you…?

Adverbs of frequency: always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never

⭐ Total: 0 / 0

📘 Let's remember!

We use adverbs of frequency to say how often we do something.

always100% · every time
usually80% · most days
often60% · many times
sometimes40% · some days
rarely20% · almost never
never0% · not ever
Where do they go?
✅ before a normal verb: I always eat fruit. / She never drinks coffee.
✅ after the verb to be (am / is / are): He is usually hungry. / We are often thirsty.
❓ Question: How often do you drink milk? → I drink milk every day. / I sometimes drink milk.

🍽️ Mia's food week

Hi! My name is Mia and I'm eleven years old. I live with my mum, my dad and my little brother, Sam. I always have breakfast at 7:30. I usually eat cereal with milk, and I sometimes have toast with jam. I never drink coffee, but I often drink orange juice.

At school, I usually have a sandwich and an apple for lunch. My friend Lucy rarely eats sandwiches. She always brings pasta in a box.

On Fridays, my family always eats pizza for dinner. My brother Sam loves sweets, but my mum never gives him sweets on school days. He only eats them at the weekend. I don't like fish, so I rarely eat it. But I love vegetables! I always eat carrots or broccoli at dinner. In the evening, I sometimes drink hot chocolate before bed.
